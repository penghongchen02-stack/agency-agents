# Late-Window Leader Study: Results (public Polymarket US data, 2026-11-20)

> **Superseded.** This run used the raw `/v1/price-history` timestamps. Those were later shown to run about 90 s early, so "T-60" decisions saw prices from after the close.
>
> The final-minute "pocket" below was an artifact of that error. See [RESULTS-2026-11-20-all-strategies.md](RESULTS-2026-11-20-all-strategies.md) for the corrected, all-asset comparison.

**Data:**
- 5,353 resolved BTC Up/Down markets (15m and 1h) from the last 45 days.
- 16,014 decision points at T-180, T-120, and T-60 s.
- 57,380 stored display-price points and 754,560 one-second settlement-index candles.
- Downloaded with `python -m pmus_recorder backfill --days 45` (no API key).

**Method:** see `pmus_recorder/study.py`. Every decision uses only data known at decision time. Trades are favourite-only (leader price $0.50–$0.98). A $0.99 price is treated as unbuyable. Each trade is 1 contract and pays the Tier 1 taker fee (θ = 0.095).

## 1. Is there anything to buy?

| Decision time | Leader buyable at ≤ $0.98 | Leader already $0.99 (no offer) | Leader won |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-180 s | 48.2% | 51.8% | 87.1% |
| T-120 s | 26.9% | 73.1% | 89.7% |
| T-60 s | **7.4%** | 92.6% | 93.7% |

## 2. Is the leader under-priced? (all 45 days, EV per contract after fee)

| Decision | Margin ≥ | Trades | Win | Avg price | EV / contract | Total (1 contract each) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-180 s | 0 bps | 2,128 | 83.7% | 0.854 | **−$0.027** | −$57.61 |
| T-180 s | 10 bps | 426 | 90.8% | 0.915 | −$0.013 | −$5.46 |
| T-120 s | 0 bps | 953 | 85.8% | 0.860 | **−$0.012** | −$10.93 |
| T-120 s | 10 bps | 134 | 94.8% | 0.901 | +$0.040 | +$5.36 |
| T-60 s | 0 bps | **57** | 98.2% | 0.900 | **+$0.075** | +$4.30 |
| T-60 s | 10 bps | 28 | 100% | 0.922 | +$0.072 | +$2.02 |

**Walk-forward** (parameters chosen on the first 30 days, tested on the last 15): T-60 s, any margin, price ≤ $0.98. That gave **15 trades**, a 100% win rate, and +$0.054 per contract, for **+$0.80 total**.

## Verdict

- **At 2–3 minutes before close there is no edge.** The leader wins about as often as its price implies, and after fees the trade **loses** 1–3¢ per contract over thousands of trades. The market prices these windows efficiently.
- **The only positive pocket is the final minute,** and it is **rare**: 57 chances in 45 days, about 1.3 a day, worth about +7.5¢ per contract.
- **That pocket is unproven:**
  - Display prices are ~1-minute samples with **no size**, so those offers may have been tiny, stale, or already taken by faster bots.
  - 57 trades is far too few to be confident.
- **Scale:** even if every one filled at 100 contracts, the total would be about $430 over 45 days before any slippage.

**Recommendation:** do **not** build a live trading bot for this strategy on the current evidence. The only way to settle the final-minute question is live order books with sizes, which need the authenticated websocket and an API key (`pmus_recorder record` with keys). Otherwise, look at a different strategy family.

> Research only. Not financial advice. Past windows do not guarantee future results.
